# Public synthetic test boundary

These fixtures are authored locally from application schemas and arithmetic.
They contain invented players, teams, IDs and statistics. No league, roster,
SportsDataverse dataset, quarantined fixture, frozen model parameter or historical
prospective record was copied into them. Matching simple integers are not a claim
of provider identity. They are test-only inputs, never production data or origins.

The original golden-master, historical corpus and real league regression results
remain separate, blocked/private validation scopes. These tests do not replace
or certify those results.
